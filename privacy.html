hfa — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-07

Who this is for

hfa is a personal alerts and household-coordination application operated by a single individual (the brand owner) for their own personal and household use. The "recipients" of any SMS sent by this system are limited to:

  • The brand owner (Garret Holland)
  • Members of the brand owner's immediate household (spouse, children)
  • A small number of close family members who have verbally consented to receive household-coordination messages (e.g., grandparents or other immediate relatives added to a kids' sports-schedule alert). The list is bounded by family relationship and is never expanded to anyone outside of family.

The current recipient count is approximately 4, with modest expansion expected over time as additional family members opt in for household coordination. There are no external users, customers, or commercial recipients.

How recipients opt in

Because the program is strictly limited to the brand owner's immediate household plus a small number of close family members, there is no public sign-up form, no SMS-keyword opt-in, and no paper sign-up. Opt-in is collected verbally and in person by the brand owner from each prospective recipient. Before the recipient's phone number is added to the application's secret store, the brand owner verbally discloses: program name (hfa), message types, approximate frequency (~30–50 msgs/month), that message and data rates may apply, STOP/HELP instructions, and the URLs of this privacy policy and the terms of service. After verbal acknowledgment, a welcome SMS is sent containing the same disclosures, giving the recipient an additional chance to reply STOP before further messages are scheduled. The full opt-in flow, the verbal disclosure script, and the welcome SMS template are documented publicly at https://garretholland.github.io/hfa-mcp-docs/opt-in.

What data is sent over SMS

The system sends short text messages to a small set of phone numbers configured in the application's secret store. Messages contain:

  1. Account-related notifications — alerts when an integrated financial service (e.g., RocketMoney, Plaid, Sequence, Google Sheets) requires the brand owner's attention. Examples: "RM scrape failed — session expired"; "stack-monitor: plaid red".
  2. Verification codes — one-time codes during re-authentication flows that the brand owner initiates from their phone.
  3. Service health alerts and periodic summaries — daily-digest excerpts, anomaly notices, structural cashflow warnings.
  4. Household coordination messages — calendar reminders, schedule changes, family logistics shared with household members.

Embedded links. Some household-coordination messages include hyperlinks to help recipients navigate to event locations, open calendar events, or read public information posted by a sports league, a tournament organizer, a school, or a parks department. Permitted link types: map locations (Google Maps via https://www.google.com/maps/… or https://maps.app.goo.gl/…; Apple Maps via https://maps.apple.com/…; Waze via https://waze.com/ul/…); calendar event links (Google Calendar via https://calendar.google.com/… or Apple iCloud-shared events); and public non-commercial webpages relevant to household logistics (e.g., a kids' sports league schedule page, a tournament bracket page, a school event or early-dismissal page, a parks-department field-status page). The program does NOT use public URL shorteners (bit.ly, tinyurl.com, etc.), affiliate or referral links, marketing or promotional links, links into any commercial product or service operated by the brand owner, or links to age-gated content. The full link policy is documented at https://garretholland.github.io/hfa-mcp-docs/opt-in.html#links-in-messages.

What data is NOT sent

  • No account numbers, full credit-card numbers, SSNs, or other sensitive PII.
  • No marketing, promotional, or commercial content of any kind.
  • No content related to lending, age-gated material, or third-party products.

How phone numbers are stored

Recipient phone numbers live in Google Secret Manager (garret-phone-number and analogous secrets for household members). They are mounted as environment variables on the Cloud Run service / jobs at runtime. Numbers are never logged, never written to disk, and never appear in scratch files, terminal output, or chat transcripts.

Data sharing

Phone numbers and message bodies are routed through Twilio, the SMS service provider, per Twilio's own privacy policy (https://www.twilio.com/legal/privacy). No data is shared with any other third party. The system does not engage in marketing, retargeting, analytics-as-a-service, advertising networks, or sale/rental of data.

Opt-out

Recipients can opt out at any time by:

  • Replying STOP to any message — Twilio honors this immediately and permanently.
  • Asking the brand owner to remove the recipient's phone number from the application configuration. Removal is immediate and irreversible without renewed verbal consent.

Replying HELP to any message returns brand-owner contact information.

SMS-specific disclosures

The following disclosures apply to all SMS messages sent by this program (required by U.S. carrier compliance / A2P 10DLC review):

  • Mobile information sharing. Mobile phone numbers and SMS opt-in data are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Phone numbers are routed only through Twilio (the SMS service provider) for the sole purpose of message delivery, per Twilio's privacy policy (https://www.twilio.com/legal/privacy).
  • Message frequency. Approximately 30–50 messages per month per recipient under normal operation (roughly one daily household-coordination message per recipient, plus up to ~20 additional account-health alerts per month directed only to the brand owner's number). Frequency may temporarily increase during exceptional circumstances (e.g., a connector outage triggering several alerts in one day).
  • Message and data rates. Message and data rates may apply per the recipient's wireless carrier plan. Recipients are responsible for any charges from their carrier.
  • HELP / STOP. Reply HELP at any time to receive brand-owner contact information. Reply STOP at any time to opt out of all future messages. Opt-out is immediate and permanent; Twilio honors STOP requests at the carrier level.
