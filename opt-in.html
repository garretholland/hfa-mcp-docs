How a recipient opts in

This program does not have a public web sign-up form, a paper sign-up form, an SMS keyword opt-in (e.g. "text JOIN to ..."), or any other publicly-accessible enrollment mechanism. The program is strictly limited to the brand owner and members of his immediate household plus close family members. There are no external users, no customers, and no marketing.

Because the recipient population is bounded by family relationship, opt-in is collected verbally and in person by the brand owner from each recipient before that recipient's phone number is added to the application's secret store. The flow is:

  1. Verbal disclosure. The brand owner verbally tells the prospective recipient (paraphrasing the script below) that they will be added to a personal alert system, what kinds of messages they will receive, the approximate message frequency, that standard message and data rates may apply, that they can opt out at any time by replying STOP or by asking the brand owner directly, and where to find the privacy policy and terms.
  2. Verbal acknowledgment. The recipient verbally agrees to receive messages.
  3. Phone number added to secret store. The brand owner adds the recipient's phone number to the application's Google Secret Manager configuration. Numbers are never logged, written to disk in plain text, or shared outside Twilio (the SMS service provider).
  4. Welcome SMS sent. A confirmation SMS (text below) is sent to the recipient. The welcome SMS contains program identification, frequency, msg & data rates language, and HELP/STOP instructions, giving the recipient an additional chance to reply STOP before any further messages are scheduled.
  5. Written record. The brand owner records the recipient's name, the last four digits of their phone number, the consent date, the consent method ("verbal, in person"), and the consent status in a private consent-records document maintained offline. Full phone numbers are never committed to a public source or shared with any third party other than Twilio.

Verbal disclosure script (read to each recipient before their number is added)

"I'm going to add your number to a personal alert system I run. It'll text you about household coordination — calendar reminders, schedule changes, kids' sports schedule, occasional family logistics. Roughly one message a day, so 30 to 50 a month. You can reply STOP at any time to opt out, or just tell me to remove your number and I will. Standard message and data rates from your carrier apply. The privacy policy and terms for the program are posted publicly at garretholland.github.io/hfa-mcp-docs."

Welcome SMS template (sent immediately after the recipient's number is added)

hfa: You're now subscribed to personal account-health and household-coordination alerts. ~30-50 msgs/month. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out. Msg&Data rates may apply.

How a recipient opts out

Recipients can opt out at any time, with immediate effect, in either of two ways:

  • Reply STOP to any message. Twilio honors STOP at the carrier level: no further messages will be delivered to that number from this program, immediately and permanently.
  • Ask the brand owner directly to remove the recipient's phone number from the application configuration (in person, by phone, by email at garret.holland@gmail.com, or by any other channel). Removal is immediate and irreversible without renewed verbal consent.

Replying HELP to any message returns brand-owner contact information so a recipient who is unsure how to opt out can reach the brand owner directly.

Messages contain no account numbers, no full credit-card numbers, no SSNs, and no other sensitive personally-identifiable information. The program sends no marketing or promotional content of any kind.

Mobile information sharing

Mobile phone numbers and SMS opt-in data are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes, and are not sold or rented under any circumstance. Phone numbers are routed only through Twilio (the SMS service provider) for the sole purpose of message delivery, per Twilio's privacy policy (https://www.twilio.com/legal/privacy).
