hfa — Terms and Conditions (SMS Notifications)

Last updated: 2026-05-01

Program name

hfa — a personal alerts and household-coordination application.

Program description

hfa sends SMS notifications to the brand owner and members of the brand owner's immediate household. Messages cover service health alerts, verification codes during re-authentication flows, periodic financial summaries, and household coordination. There are no external users, no commercial recipients, and no marketing content.

Recipient eligibility

SMS recipients are limited to:

  • The brand owner (Garret Holland)
  • Members of the brand owner's immediate household (spouse, children)
  • A small number of close family members who have verbally consented to receive household-coordination messages (e.g., grandparents or other immediate relatives added to a kids' sports-schedule alert). The list is bounded by family relationship and is never expanded to anyone outside of family.

The current recipient count is approximately 4, with modest expansion expected over time as additional family members opt in. Each recipient has given verbal consent and is added to the system manually by the brand owner. A written record (name, phone last-4, consent date, consent method) is maintained by the brand owner.

Message frequency

Approximately 30–50 messages per month per recipient under normal operation. This is roughly one daily household-coordination message per recipient, plus up to ~20 additional account-health alerts per month directed only to the brand owner's number. Frequency may temporarily increase during exceptional circumstances (e.g., a connector outage triggering several alerts in one day).

Message and data rates

Standard message and data rates may apply per the recipient's wireless carrier plan. Recipients are responsible for any charges from their carrier.

HELP instructions

Reply HELP to any message and the system will return brand-owner contact information (Garret Holland, garret.holland@gmail.com).

STOP instructions

Reply STOP to any message to opt out of all future SMS from this program. Opt-out is immediate and permanent. Re-enabling messages requires the brand owner to add the recipient back to the system, which itself requires fresh verbal consent.

Recipients may also opt out by asking the brand owner directly to remove their phone number from the application configuration.

Mobile information sharing

Mobile phone numbers and SMS opt-in data are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Phone numbers are routed only through Twilio (the SMS service provider) for the sole purpose of message delivery.

Support contact

For all support requests, questions about this program, or to opt out through a non-SMS channel: garret.holland@gmail.com.

Changes to these terms

The brand owner may update these terms from time to time. Material changes will be communicated to all current recipients via SMS before taking effect.
