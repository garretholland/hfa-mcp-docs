hfa — Terms and Conditions (SMS Notifications)

Last updated: 2026-05-07

Program name

hfa — a family-life-management application.

Program description

hfa is a family-life-management application that consolidates household finances, budgets, bills, and schedules. This SMS program sends the application's notifications to its opted-in users: service health and account/financial alerts, verification codes during re-authentication flows, periodic financial summaries, and schedule and event reminders. Some schedule/event reminders include hyperlinks — map locations (Google Maps, Apple Maps, Waze), calendar event links (Google Calendar, Apple iCloud-shared events), and public informational webpages relevant to event logistics such as sports league schedule pages, tournament bracket pages, school event pages, and parks-department field-status pages. The full link policy (permitted link shapes, prohibited shorteners, no marketing/affiliate/age-gated content) is documented on the Opt-In page. Notifications only — no marketing or promotional content.

Recipient eligibility

Every SMS recipient is a user of the application who has affirmatively opted in to receive its notifications. Each user gives consent directly, in person, at onboarding and is added to the system manually by the operator. A written record (name, phone last-4, consent date, consent method) is maintained for every recipient. The program is low-volume and notification-only; recipients may opt out at any time by replying STOP.

Message frequency

Approximately 30–50 messages per month per recipient under normal operation. This is roughly one daily schedule/event reminder per recipient, plus up to ~20 additional account-health alerts per month directed only to the brand owner's number. Frequency may temporarily increase during exceptional circumstances (e.g., a connector outage triggering several alerts in one day).

Message and data rates

Standard message and data rates may apply per the recipient's wireless carrier plan. Recipients are responsible for any charges from their carrier.

HELP instructions

Reply HELP to any message and the system will return brand-owner contact information (Garret Holland, garret.holland@gmail.com).

STOP instructions

Reply STOP to any message to opt out of all future SMS from this program. Opt-out is immediate and permanent. Re-enabling messages requires the brand owner to add the recipient back to the system, which itself requires fresh verbal consent.

Recipients may also opt out by asking the brand owner directly to remove their phone number from the application configuration.

Mobile information sharing

Mobile phone numbers and SMS opt-in data are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Phone numbers are routed only through Twilio (the SMS service provider) for the sole purpose of message delivery.

Support contact

For all support requests, questions about this program, or to opt out through a non-SMS channel: garret.holland@gmail.com.

Changes to these terms

The brand owner may update these terms from time to time. Material changes will be communicated to all current recipients via SMS before taking effect.
